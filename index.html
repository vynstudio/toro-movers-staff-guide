The call, in order

1. “Thank you for calling Toro Movers. This is Angie. How can I help you?” Then: “I just need a few details so we can give you a clear quote.”
2. “What do you need today? A full move, labor only, packing, or just one or two big items?”
3. “Is this a house, an apartment, an office, or a storage unit?” Ask it for where they leave and where they go.
4. Pickup and drop-off address, city and zip. Extra stop. Date, flexible or not. Morning or afternoon.
5. Packed or not. Piano, safe, treadmill, sectional, fridge, washer and dryer, king bed, glass table. Anything staying, dump, or donation.
6. “Our rate is $95 per hour, per mover. We confirm the crew size when we quote, and there is a 2-hour minimum. No hidden fees.” Then name, number, and if text is okay.
7. Repeat the move back: service, both places, floors, stairs or elevator, date, bedrooms, packed, phone. “Thank you for calling Toro Movers.”

Questions by place

House. Bedrooms and rooms. One story or two. Stairs inside or at the door. Truck on the driveway, street, or a long walk.
Apartment. Floor. Elevator or stairs only. Reserve the elevator. Move-in window. Loading zone. Certificate of insurance. Walk from the truck.
Storage. Size: 5x5, 5x10, 10x10, 10x20. Ground floor or upstairs. Loading in, taking out, or both.
Office. Desks. Server, copier, or anything heavy. Business hours, after hours, or weekend.

Practice call

Customer: “I need to move my 2-bedroom apartment in Kissimmee to a house in Winter Park next Saturday. Third floor, elevator, moves only from 9 to 1.”

Confirm full move. Pickup is a 3rd-floor apartment with an elevator and a 9 to 1 window. Ask loading zone, insurance certificate, and parking walk. Drop-off is a house: stories, stairs, driveway. Get both addresses, packed or not, heavy items, name, and phone. Say the rate. Do not give a total. Repeat it back.
